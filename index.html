How it works

NetShield uses Android's VPN switch only to see DNS, the "phone book" lookups every app makes before it loads anything.

An app asks

An app wants to load ads.example.com and asks for its address.

NetShield checks

Your rules first, then over a million known ad and tracker domains, in microseconds.

Ad? Dead end.

Blocked names get an empty answer, so the ad never downloads and never shows.

Everything else flows

Normal requests are answered over encrypted DNS. The rest of your traffic never touches NetShield.

Honest about the limits

DNS blocking is fast and battery friendly, but some ads can't be separated from the content they come with.

Blocks

  • In-app ads in games and free apps
  • Website ads and pop-ups in every browser
  • Trackers and analytics, including hidden ones
  • Phone telemetry from Xiaomi, Samsung, OPPO, vivo, Huawei
  • Malware and phishing domains

Can't block

  • YouTube, Instagram and Facebook ads: they come from the same servers as the videos and posts
  • Anything while Android's Private DNS names a provider (NetShield tells you)
  • Running next to another VPN: Android allows only one at a time

Install in a minute

NetShield isn't on Google Play. Install it directly, or through Ash Store to get updates automatically.

  1. Tap Download APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Open NetShield and tap Turn on. It downloads the blocklists and asks once for the VPN permission.
  4. Optional: in Android's VPN settings, turn on Always-on VPN for NetShield to keep it on after a reboot.

Questions

Is it a real VPN? Does my traffic go to a server?

No. NetShield uses Android's VPN feature only to see DNS lookups on your phone. Your browsing goes straight to the internet as usual. Only allowed lookups leave the phone, encrypted, to the DNS provider you choose.

Will it slow my phone or drain the battery?

No noticeable effect. Only tiny DNS packets pass through NetShield, and blocked ads mean pages and apps load less.

An app or site broke. What do I do?

Open the query log, find the blocked request from that app and tap Allow, everywhere or only in that app. If an app refuses to work behind any VPN (some banking apps), let it skip NetShield in Settings.

Why does Android show a key icon?

Android shows the key for every app that uses the VPN feature. With NetShield it only means DNS is being filtered on the phone.

Does it collect any data?

No account, no analytics, no ads. The query log stays on your phone. The code is open source on GitHub.
